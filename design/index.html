What PayOnce checks

Every check that makes a payment safe, in one place.

Stop paying invoices you cannot prove. PayOnce reads the invoice from the chain — hash, badge, LEI and unpaid status — then pays the payee on chain once.

⌗

Document hash match

The invoice file must match the hash stored on chain.

⬢

Badge and LEI

Seller identity is shown with its badge, and LEI state is shown when present.

✓

Pay once

The contract refuses a second payment, a wrong amount, or a changed payee.

❐

Verified receipt

Every payment gets a receipt with the transaction hash, block, and payee.
